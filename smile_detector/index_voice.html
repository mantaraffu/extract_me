emotion

first verdict after s: happy for at least % of the time with a face is praise, less is a scolding. Then every s: smiling more than in the previous window moves one step along the encouragements, less moves one step along the reprimands.

a closed list of commands (save session, reset, tell me) recognised through a Vosk grammar: anything outside the list decodes to [unk] and is ignored. tell me opens a free window where everything said is transcribed, and which closes by itself.

the audio follows the source: the microphone with the webcam, the file itself when a video is loaded. Recognition runs at 16 kHz, so a loaded video plays back dull - it is a test source, not something to listen to.

blinks, time smiling and not, emotions, hands, coach verdicts, recognised commands and free transcripts, and a per-minute timeline. Written when the page closes, every 30 s, and on demand. Needs the bundled server (npm run serve).
